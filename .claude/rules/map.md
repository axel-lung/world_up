# Map

Decided on 2026-11-22 with a Claude Design mock-up (`Carte - Monuments.dc.html`, project `57936f5d-de5d-4211-aee8-9def1a24e9c9`; unlocking a card on site follows its `Carte - Je suis ici`, `Carte - Refus Je suis ici` and `Carte - Tampon profil acces` screens). The page is `/c/heritage/map`, for signed-in players (`MapExplorer`, `src/components/map/`).

- **France by department** (`FranceMap`): the metropolitan departments and the five overseas ones as insets, each filled by the share of its visible cards the player owns, in six steps (`progressStep()`, the `--map-step-*` colors of `theme.css`). Their paths come from `scripts/build_france_map.py`; a department is a button that opens its sheet: the progress (`departmentProgress()`), "Voir les cartes" (the collection with `?tab=all&dept=21`, which `CollectionGrid` keeps in its links and shows as a chip to remove) and "Zoomer" (its bounding box).
- **The monuments** (`MonumentMap`): MapLibre, loaded on this page only. Every visible card with a point is a pin (`PinFace`): in its rarity's color when owned, greyed and dashed when lacking, with 1 to 6 dots. Supercluster gathers pins under 72 px apart into a group (`ClusterFace`, its ring the share owned) that a touch zooms into. Pins and groups are buttons put on the map as MapLibre markers (made in the map's `moveend`, the button drawn into them with a portal), so the keyboard and screen readers reach them. MapLibre's worker is bundled with `new URL('maplibre-gl/dist/maplibre-gl-worker.mjs', import.meta.url)`: its own lookup, next to its module, fails once bundled.
- **The data**: the page passes the player's owned card IDs and per-department progress; the browser fetches the monuments once per version (`/c/heritage/map/monuments?v=`, `monumentsVersion()` an md5 of what the pins show), kept for a year. A monument's sheet asks `/c/heritage/map/monuments/[id]` for what only an owned card shows: its page, thumbnail and copies. Filters (rarity, "Il me manque") and the list of the area, nearest first (`LIST_MAX` rows), are computed in the browser.
- **The background** (`src/lib/map/tiles.ts`, `style.ts`): one PMTiles archive in the OpenMapTiles schema, built with Planetiler, and the Noto Sans glyphs, in `./map` (`map/README.md`); `/map/tiles/<archive>/<z>/<x>/<y>.pbf` and `/map/fonts/...` serve them, public and cached for a year (a new archive has a new name). The style is the mock-up's palette, its dark one since the site has one theme, the vitrail (2026-11-25); the departments' steps are the vitrail's `--map-step-*`. Without an archive, the pins show on a plain ground. The map credits "© OpenMapTiles © OpenStreetMap", `/credits` in full.
- **Position**: "Autour de moi" asks the device (`navigator.geolocation`) and uses the position in the browser only, to center the map and measure distances: it is never sent. "Je suis ici" sends it once (`/privacy` says both). The Android app gets the location permissions with its next release; an older one refuses the position. "Itinéraire" opens the phone's maps app on the monument (`geo:` on Android, Apple Plans on an iPhone, OpenStreetMap elsewhere, `directions.ts`).
- **"Je suis ici"** (`VisitFlow`, one native modal `<dialog>` whose step changes): on the first visit on this device the reason for the position (remembered in `localStorage`), then the search, which watches the position (`watchPosition`, never a cached one) until one is precise enough or 20 s pass, then the server's answer: a refusal in the same bottom sheet (too far with the way there, imprecise, already visited, the day's limit, a trip too short, the position refused with how to allow it, in the app or a browser), or the card unlocked over the whole screen (it turns over, the stamp comes down, the `stamp` effect sounds). The sheet then shows the day of the visit; a visited pin wears a sage tick, and the filters gain "Visités". The stamp (`VisitStamp`) goes on the player's own cards only: `Card` and `CardRow` take `visitedAt`, which `listCollectionCards()` reads for the player's own collection only. The map sheet's history entry is pushed in a layout effect, before the flow's passive one: opened together (`?here=1`), the flow's entry stays on top.
- On a phone the map fills the screen under its own head, above the tab bar (`--map-top`, measured: the site's header from 720 px); the department's and monument's sheets rise without a shade and Back closes them; "Liste" swaps the map for the list. From 720 px a panel on the left holds the filters, the sheet and the list.
- Access: the home's menu tile, the collection's head ("Carte"), a card's page ("Voir sur la carte", `?card=<slug>`, which opens the map on it).
