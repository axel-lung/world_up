# UI

- The public and game pages sit in the `src/app/(site)/` route group, whose layout adds the header (navigation, the player's packs and coins), the footer and a player's tab bar. The sign-in pages (`(auth)`) and the back-office have their own layouts. The pack opening sits in `(play)`, with no layout: it fills the screen, and its close button leads back to the packs page (`/c/heritage/packs`: the reserve, its button and the odds).
- On a phone (under 720 px), a signed-in player gets the "thumb" layout (Claude Design's `MobileB.dc.html` and its handoff README, adopted on 2026-11-20); screens from 720 px don't change. The header and the footer are hidden: each page heads itself (the home has the bell, the reserve and the coins), and the navigation is the tab bar fixed at the bottom (`SiteTabBar`, `src/components/layout/TabBar.tsx`): Accueil, Collection, Paquets (raised, with the packs waiting), Échanges (with the offers and friend requests waiting) and Profil (`/account`). Échanges also stands for the friends and the leaderboard, which `SocialTabs` links at the top of those three pages (three equal parts on a phone, "Offres" first). The market left the bar: the collection's head and a card's page lead to it. The footer's links and the theme switch are on the profile. The bar steps aside while a text field has the focus (the keyboard is up), and on a page that sets `data-hide-tab-bar` (a card's page); what sticks to the bottom of a page takes `bottom: var(--tab-bar-offset)`. The header, the bar and the footer read the player once per request (`getPlayerStatus()`). Visitors and wider screens keep the header's links and the footer.
- A page lays out its phone and wide versions side by side, with the global `phone-only` and `wide-only` classes (`theme.css`), and the shared phone pieces in `src/app/(site)/phone.module.css` (the page's head, rows on the surface, tiles, rings, two- or three-way switches). A form is never in both: a `Sheet` with `inline` shows its content in the page from 720 px, and in a bottom sheet behind a row or a button on a phone (the profile's forms, adding a friend). `Sheet` (`src/components/ui/Sheet.tsx`) is a native modal `<dialog>`: it opens from its button and adds a history entry so that Back (the phone's too) closes it, or from the address (`closeHref`, an offer's `?offer=12`). Links inside a sheet `replace`, and the actions its forms call (resale, trades, friends) redirect with `RedirectType.replace`, so that the page they lead to takes the sheet's entry. On a phone, an action's success shows as a toast for 2.2 s (`Notice` with `toast`); errors stay in the page.
- The phone's card page (a player's) fills the screen: a bar on top (back to the list it was opened from, the card's place in it, the copies), the card between arrows that go from owned card to owned card of that list, looping, and a dock of actions (Revendre, Enchères, Échanger). The list's cards carry it in their address (`?from=/c/heritage/collection?tab=…`), and the page finds the neighbours with `ownedNeighbours()`.
- The collection's grid (`CollectionGrid`: the collection, a series, a friend's collection) opens on the cards owned (the first of `COLLECTION_TABS`; the last, "Visitées", lists the monuments visited on site, never on a friend's). Its filters apply at once, still kept in the address: the rarity as chips (links), the series as a list that submits itself (`next/form`, a button under `<noscript>`). On a phone (decided on 2026-11-25, `App Vitrail - Collection.dc.html`), the tabs are chips scrolling sideways (the duplicates' and the visits' counts on theirs), and the rarity and the series sit in one "Filtres" sheet of links behind a button, which names them once chosen; the collection page switches between the cards and the series ("Par série", `?by=series`), and offers the player's duplicates for resale in a moss banner (a confirming sheet, like the home's). The cards show as a grid or a denser list (`CardRow`), the player's choice kept in the `collection-view` cookie (`src/lib/collection-view.ts`, a display preference); until they choose, the grid, three cards a row on a phone. "Voir plus" asks for one more page (`?page=` counts the pages shown, from the first), up to `COLLECTION_MAX_PAGES`, past which the filters narrow it down: every page shown is sent again. The first card it brings takes the focus (`FocusBroughtCards`).
- The market's list works the same way: chips for the rarity and "Il me manque", lists that submit themselves for the series and the sort, "Voir plus" up to `MARKET_MAX_PAGES`; on a phone an auction is a row (`AuctionTile`: the card's vignette, its name and rarity, the bids, the time left and the viewer's part as chips, the price on the right, edged in moss while they lead and in red once outbid). A page's explanations (the market, the trades, the leaderboard's rule) fold into `MoreInfo`, a native `<details>`. The collection's home shows the 4 series furthest along and the 3 achievements nearest, the rest behind a `<details>`; on a phone (`Accueil - Refonte World Up.dc.html`), the logo, the pseudo, the flame and the bell, the reserve under a stone arch, three figures (cards, coins, rank), the menu ("Explorer") and "À faire" (the offer that expires first, the wished cards on sale, the duplicates to resell, the nearest achievement). The menu (decided on 2026-11-25, after option 3b of `Accueil - Menu global.dc.html` on 2026-11-23) is three families of four tiles, each family on a block of stone edged in its color, its name after a lozenge: "Ma collection" in parchment (Collection, Séries, Doublons, Carte), "Échanger" in moss (Marché, Mes ventes, Échanges, Amis), "Jouer" in copper (Historique, Série, Succès, Classement). A tile is its name under a lozenge of its family's glass holding the icon, without a figure; the duplicates, the offers and the friend requests show as a parchment sticker on the lozenge's top corner. The account, the rules, the odds and the credits follow as pills; the activity is the head's bell. "Série" opens the streak's screen like the flame, "Succès" the achievements' sheet (the one "À faire" opens too). The leaderboard is a list, not a table, so that it fits a phone. At the end of a pack opening, a card touched (or its button, from the keyboard) opens large in `CardZoom`, a modal `<dialog>` that goes from card to card.
- In a Drizzle select from a single table, columns interpolated into a `sql` template come out unqualified, so a correlated subquery can turn ambiguous: write such subqueries with explicit aliases (`seriesProgress()`).
- The reference design is the "vitrail" redesign (decided on 2026-11-25), in the Claude Design project `57936f5d-de5d-4211-aee8-9def1a24e9c9`: a church at dusk, walls of grey stone (`#18181b` to `#5a5a61`), stained glass in copper, moss and parchment leaded in ink (`#0c0c0e`), Cinzel for the titles and buttons, Alegreya Sans for the text, Alfa Slab One for the figures and the monuments' names. Game objects (cards, packs, rarity stickers, buttons) stay inked stickers with a sharp ink shadow. Import it through the Claude Design MCP server (log in with `/design-login`), starting from `Accueil - Refonte World Up.dc.html`, `VitrailCarte.dc.html` (the card), `VitrailTabBar.dc.html`, the `App Vitrail - *.dc.html` screens (Collection, Paquets, Marche, Echanges, Profil, Connexion) and the `Carte - * - Vitrail.dc.html` ones; `_ds/organic-31cdf777-e85b-4a4e-bbaa-571dde5117fd/` is the Organic base underneath. The World Up v2 design system (project `8508c819-…`, kept in `design/`) is superseded. The pack opening and the daily streak's screen keep the motion of `MobileB.dc.html` (its "B · Ouverture" and "B · Série" screens) in the "World Up Mobile UX" project (`04a53348-44c8-4c9f-a3fb-f3a2faf6a706`), dressed as the vitrail.
- Design tokens become CSS variables of one theme. Each component (card, button, badge, gauge, timer, purchase dialog) becomes a React component with the same variants and states as in Claude Design.
- How it is wired: `src/styles/organic.css` (the Organic stylesheet, vendored as is, imported in a cascade layer so everything else overrides it) and `src/styles/theme.css` (the vitrail's stone, glass and text, the rarity colors, the sticker look shared by every collection, and the vitrail's dress of Organic's buttons, fields, choices and tabs) are global. One theme, dark (decided on 2026-11-25, the mock-ups give the light theme the same stone): the root layout sets `data-theme="dark"` on `<html>`, so the collection's dark accent applies; there is no theme switch nor `theme` cookie any more, and a `data-theme` left on an element changes nothing. The page's ground is the stone lit by candles at its top (`--page-glow`). A collection's look lives in `collections.theme`, validated by `src/lib/collection-theme.ts`: its accent family (the same in both of its themes since `drizzle/0024_vitrail_theme.sql`: copper `#c8743c`, `#f6b877` for text, ink on its fills), the `--skin-*` variables (`--skin-color`, the pack and emblem glass) and its `emblem`, one of the icons in `src/components/collection/Emblem.tsx`. The root layout injects the default collection's variables as a `<style>`; `getActiveCollection()` and `getVisibleCard()` read the emblem, which the `Card` component takes with the collection's name. Components live in `src/components/`, as CSS modules. The stone arch with its rose window (`StoneArch`) stages what matters most on a page: the reserve (home, packs), the card (its page), the welcome page's window.
- The card (`Card`, from `VitrailCarte.dc.html`, decided on 2026-11-25): a cream sticker outlined and shadowed in ink, the photo in an arched window leaded in ink, the rarity as a tilted sticker of its color over the window's foot (its dots, and its name on a large card), the name in Alfa Slab One; a large card adds the place, the protection, the photo's credit and the series. Its measures are shares of its width (it is a size container, `cqw`): 5:7 in a grid (the pack opening's `stage-layout.ts` counts on it), 100:155 large. A card without its photo shows the window's glass in its rarity's colors; a missing one, striped glass and a dashed outline on the stone. `CardVignette` is the card at a row's size (the list view, the market, the wishlist). A card with a page (`href`) zooms and tilts under the pointer (`--hover-tilt`, the other way on every other card of the collection's grid), with no underline on its name; nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Copper (`--color-accent`) is for fills, icons and large text; running text in the accent takes `--color-accent-strong` (light copper), links `--color-link`, and text on a copper fill takes `--color-on-accent` (the ink). The main action is a raised slab of copper glass (`.btn-primary`), the others blocks of stone outlined in `--color-line`.
- Icons come from `lucide-react`, with the stroke thickened for every icon in `theme.css` (`.lucide`). Fonts (Cinzel, Alegreya Sans, Alfa Slab One) are served by `next/font` (`src/app/layout.tsx`), never from Google at runtime: `--font-heading`, `--font-body` and `--font-figure`.
- Touch screens: a tap draws no browser highlight (`theme.css`), so a control shows its own pressed state (`:active`). Hover styles go under `@media (hover: hover)`: a touch screen keeps a tapped element's hover until the next tap (`theme.css` does it for Organic's buttons).
- No zoom, on the site as in the app (decided on 2026-11-21, knowing that it departs from WCAG 1.4.4 and fails Lighthouse's `meta-viewport` audit): the root layout's `viewport` sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no`, `html` takes `touch-action: pan-x pan-y` (no pinch, no double tap) and `ZoomLock` cancels Safari's pinch, which ignores `user-scalable`. In the app, the text still follows the phone's font size.
- Sounds and vibrations (`src/lib/effects.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): the pack opening (charge, burst, a card turned and its rarity's chime, a legendary's suspense, the end) and the streak screen. The sounds are synthesized with Web Audio, from no file, so the first one must follow a touch; the vibrations go through the app's `@capacitor/haptics` plugin, or the browser's Vibration API (an app built before the plugin has none). One switch per device turns both off (`localStorage`, `useEffectsOn()`): the opening stage's button (`EffectsToggle`) and the profile's (`EffectsSwitch`).
- The announcement banner (`src/lib/announcements.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): written at `/admin/announcements` (a message, an optional link to a page of the site or an https address, an info or important tone, a start and an optional end, each publication logged), shown under the header of every `(site)` page (`SiteAnnouncement`), the latest one started and not ended. A visitor who closes it keeps its ID in the `announcement` cookie (a display preference, listed on `/privacy`), so the server leaves it out.
- While the next page loads, a terracotta bar runs along the top of the screen (`NavigationProgress`, root layout), so that a tap answers at once: it starts on a click on a link, or a GET form sent, to another page of the site, ends when the address changes, and fades in after 150 ms so that a quick page doesn't flash it. The tab bar's links show their own pending state too (`useLinkStatus`).
- `/design` is the development showcase of every component, variant and state, in both themes: check UI changes there. It returns 404 in production.
- Public card pages (`/c/heritage/cards/[slug]`) are the search-engine entry points. Each card shows its photo's author and licence with a link to the Commons file; the back shows the excerpt, the article link and "Texte : Wikipédia, licence CC BY-SA 4.0".
- Card pages read `getVisibleCard()` (`src/lib/catalog/cards.ts`): a hidden card, or one of an inactive collection, has no page (404). `/sitemap.xml` lists every visible card, built at request time from the database. `NOINDEX=1` in staging's `.env` turns `robots.txt` into a disallow-all and adds a noindex robots meta to every page, since staging is public too.
- Targets: LCP under 2.5 s on mobile 4G (lazy-loaded WebP), Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 performance and ≥ 95 accessibility on a card page, WCAG 2.1 AA. The pack opening plays by itself for 2.1 s (the pack floats in its halo, then a charge of 1 s, longer while the server answers, and a burst that tears it open) and the cards deal in within 0.7 s more, under the 3 s limit; "Tout révéler" skips to the end from the burst on. Then the player turns the cards over one by one, rarest last, each throwing off its rarity's glow, sparks and stamp (rays from a super rare up), and thrown off to the left for the next; a legendary trembles face down, harder once touched (0.75 s), then flashes and shakes the stage. At the end the cards pop up side by side. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves and the cards fade in. Its stage is darker than the stone, rays of stained glass behind, the pack a slab of copper glass with a rose window, and its layout (`stage-layout.ts`: the pack, the stack, the final grid, the sparks) adapts to `cardsPerPack`.
