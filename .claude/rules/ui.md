# UI

- The public and game pages sit in the `src/app/(site)/` route group, whose layout adds the header (navigation, the player's packs and coins), the footer and a player's tab bar. The sign-in pages (`(auth)`) and the back-office have their own layouts. The pack opening sits in `(play)`, with no layout: it fills the screen, and its close button leads back to the packs page (`/c/heritage/packs`: the reserve, its button and the odds).
- On a phone (under 720 px), a signed-in player gets the "thumb" layout (Claude Design's `MobileB.dc.html` and its handoff README, adopted on 2026-11-20); screens from 720 px don't change. The header and the footer are hidden: each page heads itself (the home has the bell, the reserve and the coins), and the navigation is the tab bar fixed at the bottom (`SiteTabBar`, `src/components/layout/TabBar.tsx`): Accueil, Collection, Paquets (raised, with the packs waiting), Échanges (with the offers and friend requests waiting) and Profil (`/account`). Échanges also stands for the friends and the leaderboard, which `SocialTabs` links at the top of those three pages (three equal parts on a phone, "Offres" first). The market left the bar: the collection's head and a card's page lead to it. The footer's links and the theme switch are on the profile. The bar steps aside while a text field has the focus (the keyboard is up), and on a page that sets `data-hide-tab-bar` (a card's page); what sticks to the bottom of a page takes `bottom: var(--tab-bar-offset)`. The header, the bar and the footer read the player once per request (`getPlayerStatus()`). Visitors and wider screens keep the header's links and the footer.
- A page lays out its phone and wide versions side by side, with the global `phone-only` and `wide-only` classes (`theme.css`), and the shared phone pieces in `src/app/(site)/phone.module.css` (the page's head, rows on the surface, tiles, rings, two- or three-way switches). A form is never in both: a `Sheet` with `inline` shows its content in the page from 720 px, and in a bottom sheet behind a row or a button on a phone (the profile's forms, adding a friend). `Sheet` (`src/components/ui/Sheet.tsx`) is a native modal `<dialog>`: it opens from its button and adds a history entry so that Back (the phone's too) closes it, or from the address (`closeHref`, an offer's `?offer=12`). Links inside a sheet `replace`, and the actions its forms call (resale, trades, friends) redirect with `RedirectType.replace`, so that the page they lead to takes the sheet's entry. On a phone, an action's success shows as a toast for 2.2 s (`Notice` with `toast`); errors stay in the page.
- The phone's card page (a player's) fills the screen: a bar on top (back to the list it was opened from, the card's place in it, the copies), the card between arrows that go from owned card to owned card of that list, looping, and a dock of actions (Revendre, Enchères, Échanger). The list's cards carry it in their address (`?from=/c/heritage/collection?tab=…`), and the page finds the neighbours with `ownedNeighbours()`.
- The collection's grid (`CollectionGrid`: the collection, a series, a friend's collection) opens on the cards owned (the first of `COLLECTION_TABS`; the last, "Visitées", lists the monuments visited on site, never on a friend's). Its filters apply at once, still kept in the address: the rarity as chips (links), the series as a list that submits itself (`next/form`, a button under `<noscript>`). On a phone, the tabs, the rarity and the series are three menus instead (État, Rareté, Série), each opening a sheet of links, and the collection page switches between the cards and the series ("Par série", `?by=series`). The cards show as a grid or a denser list (`CardRow`), the player's choice kept in the `collection-view` cookie (`src/lib/collection-view.ts`, a display preference like the theme); until they choose, a phone (`isPhoneRequest()`, the client hint or the user agent) gets the list and a wider screen the grid. "Voir plus" asks for one more page (`?page=` counts the pages shown, from the first), up to `COLLECTION_MAX_PAGES`, past which the filters narrow it down: every page shown is sent again. The first card it brings takes the focus (`FocusBroughtCards`).
- The market's list works the same way: chips for the rarity and "Il me manque", lists that submit themselves for the series and the sort, "Voir plus" up to `MARKET_MAX_PAGES`. A page's explanations (the market, the trades, the leaderboard's rule) fold into `MoreInfo`, a native `<details>`. The collection's home shows the 4 series furthest along and the 3 achievements nearest, the rest behind a `<details>`; on a phone, the reserve, three figures (cards, coins, rank), the menu and "À faire" (the offer that expires first, the wished cards on sale, the duplicates to resell, the nearest achievement). The menu (decided on 2026-11-23, option 3b of Claude Design's `Accueil - Menu global.dc.html`, project `57936f5d-de5d-4211-aee8-9def1a24e9c9`, after option 2b's mosaic of 16 tiles) is three families of four tiles raised like arcade buttons (an inked outline and drop shadow, pressed in under the finger), colored by family on their block (`data-tone`): "Ma collection" on sand (Collection, Séries, Doublons, Carte), "Échanger" on sage (Marché, Mes ventes, Échanges, Amis), "Jouer" on terracotta (Historique, Série, Succès, Classement). Each tile shows its figure under its name: the share of the collection, the series finished, what reselling the duplicates would bring, the monuments visited, the sales running and the player's own (`marketCounts()`), the offers, the friends (`countFriends()`), the packs opened (`openingCount()`), the streak's days, the achievements, the rank. Each family shows its own beside its name: the cards owned out of the total, the rank, the streak (the mock-up's "Niv. 7" has no counterpart in the game). The account, the rules, the odds and the credits follow as pills; the activity is the head's bell. "Série" opens the streak's screen like the flame, "Succès" the achievements' sheet (the one "À faire" opens too); the duplicates, the offers and the friend requests show as a sticker stuck askew on the tile's corner. It replaced the phone's 4 series in progress (the "Séries" tile leads to them). The leaderboard is a list, not a table, so that it fits a phone. At the end of a pack opening, a card touched (or its button, from the keyboard) opens large in `CardZoom`, a modal `<dialog>` that goes from card to card.
- In a Drizzle select from a single table, columns interpolated into a `sql` template come out unqualified, so a correlated subquery can turn ambiguous: write such subqueries with explicit aliases (`seriesProgress()`).
- The reference design is the "World Up v2" design system in the Claude Design project linked in the spec's "Interface" section: the Organic base (cream, terracotta, sage; Caprasimo for display, Figtree for text) with a playful tone, where game objects (cards, packs, rarity stickers) are inked stickers. Import it through the Claude Design MCP server (log in with `/design-login`), starting from `Design system World Up v2.dc.html`, `CarteV2.dc.html` (the card it imports), `_ds/organic-31cdf777-e85b-4a4e-bbaa-571dde5117fd/_ds_bundle.js`, `_ds/organic-31cdf777-e85b-4a4e-bbaa-571dde5117fd/styles.css` and `support.js`. The v1 files (`Design system World Up.dc.html`, Nocturne) are superseded. The pack opening and the daily streak's screen follow `MobileB.dc.html` (its "B · Ouverture" and "B · Série" screens) in the "World Up Mobile UX" project (`04a53348-44c8-4c9f-a3fb-f3a2faf6a706`), since 2026-11-21.
- Design tokens become CSS variables for the light and dark themes. Each component (card, button, badge, gauge, timer, purchase dialog) becomes a React component with the same variants and states as in Claude Design.
- How it is wired: `design/Design system World Up v2.dc.html` and `design/CarteV2.dc.html` are the mock-ups kept for reference. `src/styles/organic.css` (the Organic stylesheet, vendored as is, imported in a cascade layer so everything else overrides it) and `src/styles/theme.css` (grounds, text, rarity colors and the sticker look shared by every collection) are global. The theme is light by default and follows the system's dark setting; `data-theme="dark"` or `"light"` forces it on any element. The player picks Auto, Clair or Sombre in the site footer (`ThemeSwitch`): the choice lives in the `theme` cookie (`src/lib/theme-preference.ts`, a display preference, consent-exempt), which the root layout reads to set `data-theme` on `<html>`, so every page, the sign-in pages and the back-office included, renders in it with no flash. A collection's look lives in `collections.theme`, validated by `src/lib/collection-theme.ts`: its accent family per theme, the `--skin-*` variables (`--skin-color`, the pack and emblem fill) and its `emblem`, one of the icons in `src/components/collection/Emblem.tsx`. The root layout injects the default collection's variables as a `<style>`; `getActiveCollection()` and `getVisibleCard()` read the emblem, which the `Card` component takes with the collection's name. Components live in `src/components/`, as CSS modules.
- The card (`Card`, from `CarteV2.dc.html`, reworked on 2026-11-17): an inked outline, then a frame in the rarity's color that widens at the foot into a band with the rarity's name and dots, around the cream panel (photo, name, place, footer). The list view (`CardRow`) frames its thumbnail the same way. A card with a page (`href`) zooms and tilts under the pointer (`--hover-tilt`, the other way on every other card of the collection's grid), with no underline on its name; nothing moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Terracotta (`--color-accent`) is for fills, icons and large text; running text in the accent color takes `--color-accent-strong`, and text on a terracotta fill takes `--color-on-accent` (the ink), which the primary button uses instead of Organic's cream, for 4.5:1.
- Icons come from `lucide-react`, with the stroke thickened for every icon in `theme.css` (`.lucide`). Fonts are served by `next/font` (`src/app/layout.tsx`), never from Google at runtime.
- Touch screens: a tap draws no browser highlight (`theme.css`), so a control shows its own pressed state (`:active`). Hover styles go under `@media (hover: hover)`: a touch screen keeps a tapped element's hover until the next tap (`theme.css` does it for Organic's buttons).
- No zoom, on the site as in the app (decided on 2026-11-21, knowing that it departs from WCAG 1.4.4 and fails Lighthouse's `meta-viewport` audit): the root layout's `viewport` sets `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no`, `html` takes `touch-action: pan-x pan-y` (no pinch, no double tap) and `ZoomLock` cancels Safari's pinch, which ignores `user-scalable`. In the app, the text still follows the phone's font size.
- Sounds and vibrations (`src/lib/effects.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): the pack opening (charge, burst, a card turned and its rarity's chime, a legendary's suspense, the end) and the streak screen. The sounds are synthesized with Web Audio, from no file, so the first one must follow a touch; the vibrations go through the app's `@capacitor/haptics` plugin, or the browser's Vibration API (an app built before the plugin has none). One switch per device turns both off (`localStorage`, `useEffectsOn()`): the opening stage's button (`EffectsToggle`) and the profile's (`EffectsSwitch`).
- The announcement banner (`src/lib/announcements.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): written at `/admin/announcements` (a message, an optional link to a page of the site or an https address, an info or important tone, a start and an optional end, each publication logged), shown under the header of every `(site)` page (`SiteAnnouncement`), the latest one started and not ended. A visitor who closes it keeps its ID in the `announcement` cookie (a display preference, listed on `/privacy`), so the server leaves it out.
- While the next page loads, a terracotta bar runs along the top of the screen (`NavigationProgress`, root layout), so that a tap answers at once: it starts on a click on a link, or a GET form sent, to another page of the site, ends when the address changes, and fades in after 150 ms so that a quick page doesn't flash it. The tab bar's links show their own pending state too (`useLinkStatus`).
- `/design` is the development showcase of every component, variant and state, in both themes: check UI changes there. It returns 404 in production.
- Public card pages (`/c/heritage/cards/[slug]`) are the search-engine entry points. Each card shows its photo's author and licence with a link to the Commons file; the back shows the excerpt, the article link and "Texte : Wikipédia, licence CC BY-SA 4.0".
- Card pages read `getVisibleCard()` (`src/lib/catalog/cards.ts`): a hidden card, or one of an inactive collection, has no page (404). `/sitemap.xml` lists every visible card, built at request time from the database. `NOINDEX=1` in staging's `.env` turns `robots.txt` into a disallow-all and adds a noindex robots meta to every page, since staging is public too.
- Targets: LCP under 2.5 s on mobile 4G (lazy-loaded WebP), Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 performance and ≥ 95 accessibility on a card page, WCAG 2.1 AA. The pack opening plays by itself for 2.1 s (the pack floats in its halo, then a charge of 1 s, longer while the server answers, and a burst that tears it open) and the cards deal in within 0.7 s more, under the 3 s limit; "Tout révéler" skips to the end from the burst on. Then the player turns the cards over one by one, rarest last, each throwing off its rarity's glow, sparks and stamp (rays from a super rare up), and thrown off to the left for the next; a legendary trembles face down, harder once touched (0.75 s), then flashes and shakes the stage. At the end the cards pop up side by side. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves and the cards fade in. Its stage is dark in both themes, and its layout (`stage-layout.ts`: the pack, the stack, the final grid, the sparks) adapts to `cardsPerPack`.
