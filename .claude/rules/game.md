# Game rules the code must enforce

The server decides everything: pack contents, timers and balances. The browser only displays them.

The game lives in `src/lib/game/`; every function there takes the clock (`now`) and, for draws, the random source as parameters. Its rules (`game_rules`, `resale_values`, `rarity_odds`, `achievements`) are rows, seeded for `heritage` by `drizzle/0008_seed_game_rules.sql` and edited at `/admin/rules`.

- **Pack refill** is computed on read, with no scheduled job: `packs = min(max_reserve, stored + intervals elapsed since last_refill)`, and `last_refill` advances by that many whole intervals (`refill.ts`). A full reserve pauses the timer: the next pack comes one interval after the opening that takes it below the maximum. Only server time counts. Keep the clock injectable, because the acceptance tests use a mocked clock. A player's `pack_state` row is created with the signup packs on their first visit to the collection. The page's timers never add the pack themselves: at zero they ask the server for the page again (`useRefreshWhenDue()`), so that the header, the tab bar and the page show it. A player is told when their reserve is full again (decided on 2026-11-17): an opening sets `pack_state.notify_when_full`, and the worker's `packs-full` job, every minute, sends the `packs_full` notification (it opens `/open`) once `last_refill` plus one interval per missing pack has passed, and clears the flag (`notifyFullReserves()` in `packs.ts`, under the row lock an opening takes). A signup below the maximum sets it too.
- **Pack opening** (`packs.ts`) is one SQL transaction holding a per-player lock (the `pack_state` row, `FOR UPDATE`): take one pack, create the `pack_openings` row and its 5 `pack_cards`, update `user_cards`, move the daily streak on, credit `game_rules.coins_per_opening` (reason `pack_opening`, referenced by the opening's ID), grant the achievements reached. A client-sent idempotency key ensures a double click opens only one pack: the page keeps the same key until an opening succeeds. On top of the reserve, a player can't open more than `OPENINGS_PER_MINUTE` (20) packs a minute.
- **Daily streak** (`streak.ts`, pure, and `streaks.ts` for the database, decided on 2026-11-21): the Paris days in a row with a pack opened. One missed day a week (Monday to Sunday) is forgiven: it doesn't count, the streak goes on; a second one in the same week breaks it, and the next pack starts a new one. The opening moves it on, under the reserve's lock, in `pack_state` (`streak_days`, `streak_last_day`, and `streak_grace_day`, the last day forgiven, which is all a week's grace needs since days are forgiven in order). Reaching a milestone (`streak_rewards`, rows edited at `/admin/rules`, seeded by `0018_seed_streak_rewards.sql`: 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365, 500 and 1000 days, a pack each) adds its bonus packs to the reserve, even beyond its maximum: the refill then waits as for a full reserve, and the full-reserve notice is only armed while the reserve is below it. The streak shows on read (`getStreak()`): 0 once a pack today could no longer carry it on, and the week's marks are walked back from the last day played.
- **Streak repair** (decided on 2026-11-22, instead of a freeze bought in advance): on the day a streak breaks (`breakDay()`), and that day only, the player can repair it for `game_rules.streak_repair_coins_per_day` coins per day of the streak, up to `streak_repair_max_coins` (seeded 10 and 1000, edited at `/admin/rules`, published on `/rules`). The missed days the week's grace can't forgive are frozen (`pack_state.streak_frozen_days`, recent days only): they neither count nor break it. Before the day's first pack, the repair only freezes days (and records the day the grace forgave); an opening that day already started a new streak over the broken one, kept in `streak_lost_days` and `streak_lost_last_day` (`brokenStreak()`), and the repair joins the two, which can reach a milestone and its bonus packs. `repairPlayerStreak()` holds the reserve's row then the wallet, checks `availableCoins()` (a leading bid's coins stay reserved), and debits `streak_repair` referenced `"<collection ID>:<day>"`, so a form sent twice pays once. The streak screen offers it (`repairStreakAction`, `src/lib/game/streak-actions.ts`), and the home's flame wears a dot meanwhile.
- **Evening reminder** (decided on 2026-11-22): the worker's `streak-reminder` job, at 20:00 in Paris (a run caught up after midnight does nothing), sends the `streak_reminder` notification (it opens `/open`, its `data` holds the days) to the streaks of `REMINDER_MIN_DAYS` (2) days or more that break at midnight unless a pack opens today (`needsReminder()`, `remindStreaks()`). Each row is looked at once a day (`pack_state.streak_reminded_on`), under the lock an opening takes. The player turns it off on `/activity` (`pack_state.streak_reminder`). Its screen (`StreakScreen`, a full-screen modal `<dialog>` in the player's theme, Back closes it) comes at the end of the day's first opening, and from the flame on the home's head and the menu's "Série" tile on a phone (`StreakButton`, which takes the tile's face as `children`). `/rules` publishes the rule and the milestones.
- **Draw**: for each card, pick a rarity from the currently valid `rarity_odds`, then pick uniformly among the *visible* cards of that rarity, both with `crypto.randomInt` (`draw.ts`; the card is the n-th visible one in ID order). Hidden cards must never come out of a pack. Duplicates increment the quantity.
- **Odds** are integers in parts per million (a set sums to 1,000,000). Changes are dated rows, never overwritten, valid from now or later, never backdated; their history is published on `/odds`. Compare `valid_from` in SQL, as `currentOdds()` does: a timestamp written by `now()` has microseconds, which a JavaScript `Date` rounds off.
- **Virtual currency** (`coins.ts`): `coin_ledger` is append-only, the balance is the sum of its entries, and it can never go negative (`addCoins()` checks it under a per-player advisory lock). Currency is earned from free pack openings, duplicate resale, achievements and auction sales, and spent at auctions, whose fee is destroyed, and on streak repairs, which destroy theirs, so that coins don't only ever grow. A paid pack that paid coins too would sell currency: ask the lawyer before lot 4 gives it any. A ledger entry is unique per player, reason and reference, so a resale form sent twice (same key, generated when the page renders) credits once, and an achievement pays once. Reselling always leaves the player one copy.
- **Achievements** are rows: a threshold on distinct visible cards, packs opened, cards of a rarity or completed series. Their titles come from `messages/fr.json` (`Achievements`), built from the kind and threshold.
- **Badges** (`badges.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): each achievement reached is a badge, an inked sticker (`BadgeSticker`) with its kind's icon, colored by its tier: its rank among its kind's achievements laid on the rarity ladder, the last one legendary (`badgeTiers()`); an achievement for a rarity takes that rarity. The profile shows them all (the missing ones greyed), a friend's collection shows theirs, and the player picks one (`featured_badges`, an achievement they have) shown beside their pseudo on the leaderboard, the friends list and a friend's page. The visits' badges are the exception (see "On-site visits").
- **Wishlist** (`wishlist.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): `wishlist_items` rows, visible cards the player lacks, at most `WISHLIST_MAX` (100) a collection, an anti-abuse code constant like `MAX_FRIENDS` (`addWish()` checks it under a per-player advisory lock). A wish counts on read only while its card is visible and still lacking. A card obtained leaves the list in the transaction that brings it (`fulfilWishes()`): `openPack()` (which sets `pack_cards.wished`, so that the reveal says "Souhait exaucé !") and `moveCard()` (trades, auctions). `createAuction()` tells the players who wish for the card (`notifyWishers()`, the `auction_wished` notification), not the seller nor a banned player, at most once per card in `WISHED_AUCTION_NOTICE_HOURS` (24), so a sale withdrawn and put up again rings once. The heart (`WishButton`, `src/components/wishlist/`, through `setWishAction` in `wishlist-actions.ts`) turns at once with `useOptimistic`, then the action's `refresh()` draws the page again; a refusal floats a message. It sits on the cards the player looking lacks: `listCollectionCards()` reads `wished` and, on a friend's collection, `viewerOwns`, and `toCollectionCards()` makes `wish` (null for no heart); `Card` and `CardRow` take it as `action`. A card's page offers it as a button, and in the phone's dock in place of "Revendre" when the card isn't owned. `/c/heritage/wishlist` lists the wishes with their running auctions (`auctionsOfCards()` in `market.ts`, which `wishlist.ts` mustn't import: `auctions.ts` imports it) and the friends who own each card, a link asking them for it in a trade; the market filters on it (`?wished=1`) and the home's "À faire" counts the wished cards on sale (`wishesOnSale()`). Friends see each other's list: their page counts their wishes the viewer owns, and `tradeableCards()` puts first the cards the other side wishes for. `/privacy` and `/rules` say so; the account export holds it.
- **On-site visits** (`visit.ts`, pure, and `visits.ts`, decided on 2026-11-22): "Je suis ici" on a monument's map sheet (or its card's page, through `?here=1` on the map) sends the device's position once to `visitAction` (`visit-actions.ts`), which checks it and forgets it: neither stored nor logged (the action logs Postgres's message only, since Drizzle's repeats the query's values). `visitMonument()`, under a per-player advisory lock, takes a visible card with a point, refuses it when the player already visited it (once per monument, ever: `card_visits` is keyed on player and card), when the position's accuracy is worse than `game_rules.visit_max_accuracy_metres` (100) or it lies farther than `visit_radius_metres` (200) from the point, widened to the point's `location_precision` up to `MAX_VISIT_RADIUS_METRES` (2000, a code constant), when the player already made `visits_per_day` (5) visits that Paris day, or when the trip from the last monument visited (in any collection, both radii deducted) would beat `MAX_TRAVEL_KMH` (900). Otherwise it records the card and the moment (`card_visits.visited_at`, which the speed check needs), adds a copy that counts and trades like a pack's (leaving the wishlist) and grants the achievements. The three rules are edited at `/admin/rules` and published on `/rules`. A refusal counts as nothing. Visits are private: a friend sees how many (`visitCount()`), never which; the stamp, the "Visitées" tab and the map's tick are the player's own. The visits' achievements (`monuments_visited`, `departments_visited`, `visits_in_a_day`, `rarities_visited`, `overseas_visited`, seeded by `0023_seed_visit_achievements.sql`, counted by `visitCounts()`) are badges in their own section of the profile, never featured beside the pseudo nor shown on a friend's page (`isVisitKind()`). `drizzle/0022` made `achievement_kind` again rather than `ADD VALUE`: Drizzle runs the pending migrations in one transaction, where an added value can't be used yet. GPS spoofing can't be prevented on Android, only limited by these rules.
- **Collection views** show visible cards only, even those a player already owns. A card the player lacks shows only its name and rarity, greyed and leading nowhere: the server sends neither its photo, its place nor its address (`toCollectionCard()`). Legendaries and Ultra rares included, since 2026-11-17 (they used to be masked, name included). A friend's collection shows the cards they own, Legendaries included. The map is the exception (decided on 2026-11-22): it places every visible card, lacking ones too, with their name and rarity, but still without their photo or address.
- **Leaderboard** (`leaderboard.ts`): distinct visible cards, ties broken by Legendaries, then Ultra rares, and so on; equal counts share a rank (rule confirmed on 2026-10-20). Banned players are left out.
- Series progression = distinct owned cards / visible cards in the series, recalculated on each opening.
- **Openings history** (`history.ts`, `/c/heritage/packs/history`, linked from the packs page): the player's openings by Paris day, newest first, each with its cards' thumbnails in reveal order and its new ones marked; a card hidden since shows its name and rarity only. "Voir plus" goes up to `HISTORY_MAX_PAGES`; the account export holds them all.
- **Staying out of gambling law**: add nothing that gives cards or currency a monetary value (no buying currency, no cash-out, no account transfer). Odds stay public, with their history. Trades and auctions make cards transferable between accounts: while every pack is free there is no "sacrifice financier", hence no JONUM, but paid packs would make the cards "objets numériques monétisables" (loi SREN, art. 40; decree 2026-60). Decided on 2026-10-22: they open without waiting for the lawyer, who examines the combination with payments before lot 4.
